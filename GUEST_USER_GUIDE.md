# NLEC Room Booking System — Guest User Guide

---

## Getting Started

**Website:** https://booking.nlec.org.au

Open the link in any web browser on your phone or computer.

---

## Step 1 — Log In

On the login screen, select **Use Access Code**.

Enter the access code provided to you and press **Sign In**.

> If you do not have an access code, please contact the NLEC Office Admin.

---

## Step 2 — View the Room Schedule

Once logged in, you will see the room schedule for today.

- The **left panel** lists all available rooms. You can tick or untick rooms to show or hide them.
- Use the **← →** arrows to navigate to a different date.
- Use the **Today** button to return to today's schedule.
- Existing bookings are shown as coloured blocks on the calendar.
- Bookings marked with ⏳ are pending approval.

---

## Step 3 — Submit a Booking Request

To request a room:

1. Click and drag on the calendar to select your desired time slot.
2. A booking form will appear. Fill in the **Event Title** and an optional description.
3. Adjust the start and end time if needed, then press **Next**.
4. Enter your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Contact Number**.
5. Press **Submit Request**.

You will receive a confirmation email shortly after submitting.

---

## Step 4 — After You Submit

Your request will be reviewed by the NLEC admin team. You will receive an email once your booking has been **approved** or if there are any issues with your request.

> Please note: submitting a request does not guarantee the booking. The room is confirmed only after you receive an approval email.
