# Rollout Email

**To:** All Staff and Ministry Leaders
**Subject:** New Room Booking System Now Live

---

Dear All,

We are pleased to announce that the NLEC Room Booking System is now live and available for use.

**How to Access**

You can access the system at: https://booking.nlec.org.au

**Signing In**

- If you have an `@nlec.org.au` email address, you can sign in directly using your NLEC Microsoft account by selecting **Sign in with NLEC Email**.
- If you do not have an NLEC email address, please use the access code provided to you to log in.

**Requesting a Room**

Once logged in, you can view the room schedule and submit a booking request by clicking and dragging on the calendar to select your preferred time slot. Fill in the event details and submit your request. You will receive an email confirmation once your request has been received, and another email once it has been reviewed.

**Need Help?**

- For **booking enquiries or general questions**, please contact **Fanny Liu** (Office Admin) at **0400 984 844**.

We hope this new system makes room bookings easier and more efficient for everyone. Thank you for your patience and support.
